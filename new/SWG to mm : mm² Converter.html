Instantly convert Standard Wire Gauge (SWG) size to metric diameter and cross-sectional area.

Select an SWG size and press Convert.

Conversion Basis
Conversion uses standard values for solid conductor sizes defined by the Imperial SWG system. Diameters are converted from inches to $\text{mm}$, and area is calculated as $\pi \cdot (D/2)^2$.
