DC Voltage Drop Calculator

Determine the minimum AWG wire size required based on system requirements and maximum allowable voltage drop.

Enter your system parameters above and calculate.

Calculation Formula
This DC calculation uses the formula: $V_{drop} = \frac{2 \cdot I \cdot L \cdot R}{1000}$, where $R$ is the wire resistance in $\Omega / 1000 \text{ft}$ (based on $\text{AWG}$ copper at $\text{20}^\circ\text{C}$).
